    这篇文章要回答一个问题：当你不确定自己记得对不对的时候，大脑里是"什么"在表示这份不确定？ The paper asks one question: when you are unsure whether you remember something correctly, what in the brain represents that uncertainty?

    作者提出可能存在两种格式。第一种，记忆本身就以概率分布的形式存着，分布的宽度就是不确定性的来源——这是"表征精度"。第二种，大脑额外维护一个标量，像一根信心温度计的读数，不携带具体记了什么，只表示总体把握。他们用脑电找到了前者在 alpha 频段、后者在 beta 频段的证据。 They propose two possible formats. First, the memory itself may be stored as a probability distribution whose width is the source of uncertainty — representational precision. Second, the brain may maintain an extra scalar, a confidence thermometer reading that carries no information about what was remembered, only overall certainty. Using EEG, they found the first in the alpha band and the second in the beta band.

    Alpha · 8–13 Hz

    概率性的记忆表征A probabilistic mnemonic code

    记忆内容可以从 alpha 功率中解码出来；而且解码得到的后验分布越宽，被试随后报告的不确定性越大。Memory content is decodable from alpha power, and the wider the decoded posterior, the more uncertain the participant later reports being.

    Beta · 14–30 Hz

    标量的信心信号A scalar confidence signal

    beta 功率直接预测不确定性报告的数值，而且这个信号整场都存在——刺激出现之前就有，并且能"预测"上一试次的不确定性。Beta power predicts the reported uncertainty value directly. The signal is present throughout the trial — even before stimulus onset — and predicts the previous trial's uncertainty.

    背景：先弄懂六个概念Background: six ideas you need first

    这一章是投资。原文的引言部分默认你已经知道这些，所以写得非常压缩。下面把每个概念展开到你读原文不会卡住的程度。 This chapter is an investment. The paper's introduction assumes you already know these and is written very densely. Here each one is opened up enough that the original won't stall you.

    ① 工作记忆（Working memory）① Working memory

    指在刺激消失之后，把信息"端在手上"维持几秒的能力。它是所有需要"记住刚才看到什么再做决定"的行为的基础。 The ability to hold information "in hand" for a few seconds after a stimulus disappears. It underlies any behaviour that requires remembering what you just saw before deciding.

    关键在于：这份维持是有噪声的。同一个刺激重复呈现，记忆的准确度每次都不一样——这就是本文的出发点。原文用 "inherently noisy and variable" 描述这一点，并引用 Fougnie 等 (2012)、van den Berg 等 (2012) 证明编码精度本身的波动就能解释工作记忆的容量限制。 Crucially, maintenance is noisy. Present the same stimulus repeatedly and accuracy differs every time — this is the paper's starting point. The original calls it "inherently noisy and variable" and cites Fougnie et al. (2012) and van den Berg et al. (2012) showing that fluctuations in encoding precision itself account for working memory limits.

    ② 表征精度（Representational precision）② Representational precision

    这是全文最核心的概念，务必分清它和"准确度"的区别。 This is the paper's central concept. Be careful to separate it from "accuracy".

    • 准确度（accuracy）：你记的位置离真实位置偏了多远。可以有一个方向性的偏差。Accuracy — how far off your remembered location is. It can have a directional bias.
    • 精度（precision）：你的记忆有多集中。精度低意味着分布很散，但你未必偏向某个方向。Precision — how concentrated your memory is. Low precision means a spread-out distribution, but not necessarily biased in any direction.

    打个比方：往靶子上扔飞镖。准确度是飞镖群的中心离靶心多远；精度是这堆飞镖本身散得有多开。本文关心的是后者——因为"散得开"正是"不确定"的数学含义。 Think of darts. Accuracy is how far the centre of the cluster is from the bullseye; precision is how tightly the darts themselves cluster. The paper cares about the latter, because "spread out" is exactly what uncertainty means mathematically.

    在概率性编码的框架下，大脑维持的不是一个点估计（"位置在 137°"），而是一整个概率分布（"最可能是 137°，但 130°–145° 也有可能"）。这个分布的宽度，就是表征精度；宽度越大，表征越不精确。 In a probabilistic coding framework, the brain maintains not a point estimate ("the location is at 137°") but a whole probability distribution ("most likely 137°, but 130°–145° is plausible too"). That distribution's width is the representational precision, and a wider distribution means a less precise representation.

    ③ 不确定性 vs 信心（Uncertainty / Confidence）③ Uncertainty vs confidence

    两者是同一枚硬币的两面：不确定性高 ≈ 信心低。理论上它们可以是不同的量（Pouget 等 2016 专门论证过），但在本文这样的连续估计任务里，作者明确声明不区分这两个词（原文："we do not distinguish between the two terms for the present continuous estimation task"）。你读的时候不必纠结用词。 They are two faces of one coin: high uncertainty ≈ low confidence. Theoretically they can be distinct quantities (Pouget et al. 2016 argue exactly this), but in a continuous estimation task like this one the authors explicitly decline to distinguish them (original: "we do not distinguish between the two terms for the present continuous estimation task"). Don't get stuck on the wording.

    ④ 元认知与元记忆（Metacognition / Metamemory）④ Metacognition / metamemory

    "关于自己认知的认知"——你不仅记得东西，还能评估自己记得多好。评估记忆质量的那部分叫元记忆。本文标题里的 "confidence"、摘要里的 "metacognitive access to working memory"，说的都是这件事。 "Cognition about one's own cognition" — you don't just remember; you can also judge how well you remember. The part that judges memory quality is metamemory. The paper's "confidence" and "metacognitive access to working memory" both refer to this.

    为什么这重要：如果大脑无法追踪自己的不确定性，人就无法做出适应性行为——比如决定"这个我拿不准，得再看一眼"。 Why it matters: without tracking its own uncertainty, an organism could not behave adaptively — deciding, say, to check again because it isn't sure.

    ⑤ 神经振荡与频段（Neural oscillations）⑤ Neural oscillations and frequency bands

    脑电记录到的是大量神经元同步活动产生的节律性电位波动。按频率划分成几个经典频段，每个频段被认为对应不同的计算功能（Fries 2015; Buzsáki & Vöröslakos 2023）。本文只涉及三个： EEG records rhythmic voltage fluctuations produced by the synchronised activity of large neuronal populations.     ⑥ 概率性群体编码与贝叶斯解码⑥ Probabilistic population codes and Bayesian decoding

    这是理解第 04 章的技术前提。核心思路是：大脑用一大群神经元的整体活动模式来表示一个变量，而不仅仅是一个神经元的放电率。这样的群体活动天然地对应一个概率分布——分布的峰值是"最可能的值"，宽度是"有多不确定"（Ma 等 2006; Jazayeri & Movshon 2006）。 This is the technical prerequisite for chapter 04. The core idea: the brain represents a variable through the collective activity pattern of a population, not a single neuron's firing rate. Such population activity naturally maps onto a probability distribution — its peak is the most likely value, its width is how uncertain (Ma et al. 2006; Jazayeri & Movshon 2006).

    研究者做的事情叫解码（decoding）：反过来，从观测到的一堆电极活动，推断出"被试最可能在记哪个位置，以及这个推断有多不确定"。本文用的是 TAFKAP 算法（van Bergen & Jehee 2019, 2021）——先建立"位置 → 脑电模式"的生成模型，再用贝叶斯定理反推"脑电模式 → 位置的后验分布"。 What the researchers do is decoding: working backwards from a set of electrode activities to "which location is the participant most likely holding, and how uncertain is that inference". This paper uses the TAFKAP algorithm (van Bergen & Jehee 2019, 2021): build a generative model of "location → EEG pattern", then invert it with Bayes' rule to get "EEG pattern → posterior over locations".

    实验范式：怎么让被试"报告不确定性"The paradigm: getting people to report uncertainty

    这是本文设计上最聪明的地方。不确定性是主观的，你怎么让被试把它变成一个可以统计分析的数字？答案是赌局。 This is the cleverest part of the design. Uncertainty is subjective — how do you turn it into a number you can analyse? The answer is a bet.

    奖励机制：为什么弧长等于不确定性The reward rule: why arc length equals uncertainty

    规则是这样的。被试画完弧线之后，真实目标位置会以反馈点的方式显示出来： Here is the rule. After the participant draws the arc, the true target location is revealed as a feedback dot:

    • 如果真值落在弧内，得分为 100e−0.08d，其中 d 是弧的长度（度）。If the true location falls inside the arc, points awarded are 100e−0.08d, where d is the arc length in degrees.
    • 如果真值落在弧外，得 0 分。If it falls outside, zero points.

    这个函数随 d 单调递减，意味着弧画得越短，潜在奖励越高——但弧越短，真值落在里面的概率也越低。于是被试陷入一个权衡： The function decreases monotonically in d, so a shorter arc means a higher potential reward — but a shorter arc is also less likely to contain the target. The participant faces a trade-off:

    • 画太短 → 拿高分的概率太低，期望收益反而小。Too short → the chance of scoring at all becomes too low.
    • 画太长 → 覆盖是稳了，但每题都只拿很少的分。Too long → you almost always score, but very little each time.
    • 最优策略：正好按自己真实的不确定性来定弧长。Optimal strategy: set the arc exactly to your own true uncertainty.

    这就是所谓的激励相容（incentive-compatible）设计：只有当被试如实报告时收益才最大，所以弧长可以被当作一份可信的、量化的不确定性自陈。原文在 STAR Methods 里写明这套设计"旨在激励校准良好的不确定性报告"，并引用了本实验室先前的工作（Li 等 2021；Honig 等 2020；Yoo 等 2018）。 This is an incentive-compatible design: payoffs are maximised only by honest reporting, so arc length can be treated as a trustworthy, quantified self-report of uncertainty. The STAR Methods state the reward was designed "to incentivize well-calibrated uncertainty reports", citing the lab's earlier work (Li et al. 2021; Honig et al. 2020; Yoo et al. 2018).

    行为结果：先证明这个任务真的有效Behavioural results: first, show the task actually works

    在动脑电之前，作者先验证了三件事（原文 Figure 1B–1E）： Before touching the EEG, the authors checked three things (Figure 1B–1E):

    • 记忆误差以目标位置为中心分布，可用 von Mises 分布很好拟合 Fig 1B——说明被试在认真做任务，没有系统性偏差。Memory errors centre on the true location and fit a von Mises distribution well Fig 1B — participants were engaged, with no systematic bias.
    • 记忆相当精确 Fig 1C——只有一个目标、延迟又短，符合预期。Memory was quite precise Fig 1C — expected with a single target and a short delay.
    • 关键：报告的弧长随记忆误差的增大而增大 Fig 1D, 1E——这条才真正说明被试对自己的记忆质量有元认知访问能力。The key one: reported arc length grows with the size of the memory error Fig 1D, 1E — this is what shows participants genuinely have metacognitive access to their own memory quality.

    两个容易被忽略但很关键的设计Two design details that are easy to miss

    其一，为什么要"打乱 run 身份"而不是完全打乱？因为不确定性报告存在序列依赖（当前试次和上一试次相关，这正是本文的发现之一）。如果完全随机打乱，就会破坏这种真实的时间结构，置换检验的零分布就不对了。保留 run 内顺序、只打乱 run 的归属，可以在保留时间结构的前提下破坏"脑电与报告的真实对应"。这个细节说明作者很清楚自己数据的结构。 First, why shuffle run identity rather than fully shuffling? Because uncertainty reports show serial dependence (current trial correlates with previous — one of this paper's own findings). A full shuffle would destroy that real temporal structure and make the null distribution wrong. Preserving within-run order while shuffling run assignment breaks the genuine EEG-to-report correspondence while keeping the temporal structure. The authors clearly understood their own data.

    其二，散点图里的数据点不是试次。你在 Figure 1D、2D、2F 里看到的每个点，是一个被试的一个分箱，不是单个试次。做法是：先按 x 轴变量把每个被试的试次排序、等分成 8 箱，取每箱的均值；然后把每个被试的数值做均值中心化，再汇总。这么做是为了让图好看且不被个体差异淹没——但代价是图上的相关强度和统计检验的相关强度不是一回事。统计检验用的是逐试次的相关系数。这个区别在 STAR Methods 的 "Quantification and statistical analysis" 里写得很明确。 Second, the dots in the scatter plots are not trials. Each dot in Figure 1D, 2D or 2F is one bin from one participant, not a single trial. Procedure: sort each participant's trials by the x-axis variable, split into 8 equal bins, take each bin's mean; mean-centre each participant's values; then pool. This makes the figure readable and stops individual differences swamping it — but the cost is that the correlation strength shown in the plot is not the one that was statistically tested. The test used trial-wise correlations. The STAR Methods spell this out under "Quantification and statistical analysis".

    读图提醒Reading caution

    看到本文散点图上的 r 值或拟合线，不要直接和正文的 t 值对照——它们来自不同的计算层级。正文报告的 t(13) 才是推断统计的结果。Do not read an r or a fit line off a scatter plot here and compare it to the t values in the text — they come from different computational levels. The reported t(13) is the inferential result.

    关于"留一 run 交叉验证"On leave-one-run-out cross-validation

    所有解码和回归都用了同一个方案：用 19 个 run 训练，在第 20 个 run 上测试，轮换 20 次。这不是可选的细节——如果在同一批数据上训练又测试，模型总能"记住"答案，得到的相关性是虚高的。STAR Methods 里写明 20 个 run 对应 20 折。 Every decoding and regression analysis uses the same scheme: train on 19 runs, test on the 20th, rotating 20 times. This is not optional detail — if you train and test on the same data, the model simply memorises the answers and the resulting correlation is inflated. The STAR Methods confirm 20 runs give 20 folds.

    结果一：alpha 承载概率性的记忆表征Result 1: alpha carries a probabilistic memory code

    这一章对应原文的 Figure 2 和 Figures S1–S3。论证分三步走，每一步都有独立的统计支持。 This chapter covers Figure 2 and Figures S1–S3. The argument proceeds in three steps, each with its own statistical support.

    第一步：记忆内容能不能从 alpha 里解码出来？Step 1: is memory content decodable from alpha at all?

    能。平均绝对解码误差为 80.5°，显著低于 90° 的机会水平： Yes. Mean absolute decoding error was 80.5°, significantly below the 90° chance level:

    t(13) = 6.42, p < 0.001

    在 14 名被试中有 12 名的解码误差在 0° 附近聚集，说明这不是个别被试驱动的效应（Fig 2B；个体结果见 Figure S1）。而且带符号的解码误差与带符号的记忆误差显著相关（Fig 2D, 2E）——也就是说，当解码器"猜偏"的方向和程度与被试真实记错的方向和程度一致时，才有资格说这个解码反映的是真实的记忆内容。 In 12 of 14 participants decoding errors clustered near 0°, so the effect is not driven by a few individuals (Fig 2B; individual results in Figure S1). And the signed decoding error correlated with the signed memory error (Fig 2D, 2E) — the decoder's errors moved in the same direction and magnitude as the participant's own errors, which is what licenses the claim that it reflects real memory content.

    为什么 80.5° 算是"解码成功"？Why is 80.5° a success?

A decoder guessing blindly would average 90° error. 80.5° sounds close to 90°, but across 14 participants that gap produces t = 6.42 — a very robust shift. This is how decoding effects on circular variables behave; linear intuition does not transfer.

    第二步：解码出的不确定性，能预测被试报告的不确定性吗？Step 2: does decoded uncertainty predict reported uncertainty?

    能，这是本文第一条主线的核心结果： Yes — this is the core result of the paper's first主线:

    t(13) = 2.32, p = 0.04　·　置换检验 p = 0.002

    也就是说：从 alpha 功率解码出来的后验分布越宽，被试随后画的弧越长。记忆表征自身携带的概率信息可以直接用于元认知判断。 In plain terms: the wider the posterior decoded from alpha power, the longer the arc the participant then drew. The probabilistic information in the memory representation itself feeds metacognitive judgement.

    第三步：解码器"行为良好"吗？Step 3: is the decoder well behaved?

    这是一个很讲究的检验。一个真正在做概率推断的解码器应该有个性质：当它输出的不确定性越大时，它的解码误差也应该越大。因为"我不确定"和"我容易错"本该是一回事。本文确实观察到了这个关系： A nice check. A decoder genuinely doing probabilistic inference should have a property: when its stated uncertainty is larger, its decoding error should also be larger — because "I'm unsure" and "I tend to be wrong" ought to be the same thing. The paper does observe this:

    t(13) = 3.16, p = 0.008

    原文称这是 "a signature for a well-behaved probabilistic decoder"（一个行为良好的概率解码器的标志）。做解码研究的人经常忘了做这个检验，本文把它当成正面证据来报告，值得学习。 The original calls this "a signature for a well-behaved probabilistic decoder". Decoding studies often skip this check; here it is reported as positive evidence — worth learning from.

    最反直觉的一条：beta 不只在"当下"The most counter-intuitive part: beta is not only about now

    行为数据先给了一个线索：不确定性报告存在序列依赖——当前试次报告的弧长与上一试次显著相关（Fig 3C）。也就是说，人的信心不是每试次独立重新计算的，它带着"上一把的手感"。 A behavioural clue comes first: uncertainty reports show serial dependence — this trial's arc length correlates with the previous trial's (Fig 3C). Confidence is not recomputed independently each trial; it carries over.

    接下来是全文最漂亮的一个分析，也是最能说明 beta 性质的一个：作者把上一试次的弧长当作因变量，用当前试次的 beta 活动去"预测"它（Fig 3D）。结果是显著的。这在逻辑上很关键——当前试次的脑电不可能由上一试次的行为造成，所以这个结果只能理解为：beta 活动反映的是一个跨越试次的、持续存在的内部状态，而不是对本试次刺激或反应的即时编码。 Then comes the paper's most elegant analysis, and the one that best characterises beta: the authors take the previous trial's arc length as the dependent variable and "predict" it from the current trial's beta activity (Fig 3D). It works. Logically this matters — current-trial EEG cannot be caused by previous-trial behaviour, so the only reading is that beta reflects a persistent internal state spanning trials, not an online encoding of this trial's stimulus or response.

    结果三：时间进程与皮层来源的分化Result 3: time courses and cortical origins diverge

    前面证明了"两个信号存在"。这一章（对应 Figure 4、S4、S5）回答"它们像不像同一个东西的两种说法"——答案是不像，而且差别很大。 The previous chapters established that two signals exist. This one (Figure 4, S4, S5) asks whether they are two descriptions of one thing. They are not, and the differences are large.

    时间进程：一个"晚"，一个"全天候"Time course: one is late, one is always on

    作者做了逐时间点的分析（−1 s 到 2.5 s，相对刺激起始），然后对"这个时间点的相关性是否显著"做聚类置换校正。结果形成鲜明对比： The authors ran a time-resolved analysis (−1 s to 2.5 s relative to stimulus onset) and applied cluster-based permutation correction to "is the correlation at this time point significant". The contrast is sharp:

    • alpha 解码出的不确定性在刺激出现后迅速下降（说明信息进来了），整个延迟期都保持可解码；但"解码不确定性 ↔ 报告不确定性"这条相关只在延迟期中后段的一个较短窗口才显著（Fig 4A, 4B）。alpha Decoded uncertainty drops quickly after stimulus onset (information arrived) and stays decodable throughout the delay; but the decoded-uncertainty ↔ reported-uncertainty correlation emerges only in a short, relatively late window in the middle of the delay (Fig 4A, 4B).
    • beta 的 ridge 预测在整个试次都高于机会水平，甚至在刺激出现之前（Fig 4D）。这是判断 beta 是"持续状态"而非"反应"的决定性证据。beta Ridge prediction is above chance across the whole trial — even before stimulus onset (Fig 4D). This is the decisive evidence that beta is a persistent state rather than a response.

    另一个值得注意的对照：当同样的贝叶斯解码器被用在时域电压（而不是 alpha 功率）上时，位置只在刺激呈现期可解码，一到延迟期就掉到机会水平（Figure S4B）。这说明 alpha 功率携带的是刺激消失之后仍然持续的记忆信息，而时域诱发电位只反映当下的感觉输入。 One more telling contrast: when the same Bayesian decoder was applied to the time-domain voltage rather than alpha power, location was decodable only during stimulus presentation and fell to chance as soon as the delay began (Figure S4B). Alpha power carries mnemonic information that persists after the stimulus is gone; the time-domain evoked response only reflects the sensory input of the moment.

    皮层来源：两个信号来自不同地方Cortical origins: the two signals come from different places

    用 sLORETA 把头皮信号投影回皮层，然后在每个皮层顶点上做逐试次相关： sLORETA projects the scalp signal back onto the cortex, then a trial-wise correlation is computed at every cortical vertex:

    • alpha 功率与目标位置相关的区域：左侧顶枕沟（parieto-occipital sulcus）的一个团簇，与 V3AB 和 IPS0 大致重叠——这正是作者此前用 fMRI 做同类任务时，发现位置解码最强、解码不确定性与行为不确定性关系最强的区域（Li 等 2021, 2025, 2026）。alpha Power correlated with target location in a cluster on the left parieto-occipital sulcus, broadly overlapping V3AB and IPS0 — the same regions where the authors' earlier fMRI work with a similar task found the strongest location decoding and the strongest link between decoded and reported uncertainty (Li et al. 2021, 2025, 2026).
    • beta 功率与报告弧长相关的区域：中央前沟、中央后沟，以及楔前叶、中央旁小叶和前额叶的若干内侧团簇（Fig 4E）。beta Power correlated with reported arc length at the precentral and postcentral sulci, plus medial clusters around the precuneus, paracentral lobule and prefrontal cortex (Fig 4E).
    • 对 alpha 做了同样的分析，但没有任何皮层源与报告弧长相关。这条阴性结果很重要——它说明两条线的分离不只是"哪个频段在哪段时间有信号"，而是空间上也分开了。The same analysis on alpha found no cortical source correlating with reported arc length at all. This null matters: the two lines are separated not only in time and band but in space.
    为什么 beta 的定位会引出麻烦Why beta's localisation creates a problem

    中央前/后沟是感觉运动皮层。而被试报告弧长需要按键。这就产生了一个煞有介事的替代解释：beta 反映的可能只是"我要按多少次键"的运动准备活动，而不是任何与信心有关的东西。这不是吹毛求疵——它足以推翻整篇文章的第二条主线。作者为此做了四项控制分析，见下一章。The pre/postcentral sulcus is sensorimotor cortex, and reporting arc length requires key presses. That yields a serious alternative explanation: beta might reflect nothing more than motor preparation for the key presses, with no relation to confidence at all. This is not nitpicking — it would sink the paper's second main claim. The authors ran four control analyses, in the next chapter.

    控制分析：证明不是肌肉在说话Controls: showing it isn't the muscles talking

    这一章对应 Figure S5，是全文方法论上最值得学习的部分。作者没有回避"beta 可能是运动信号"这个质疑，而是设计了一组互相独立的检验来逐一排除。读原文时如果跳过补充材料，你会错过这篇文章最扎实的一段论证。 This chapter covers Figure S5 and is the paper's most instructive methodological passage. Rather than dodging the "beta might be motor" objection, the authors designed a set of independent checks against it. Skip the supplemental material and you miss the most solid stretch of reasoning in the paper.

    四项控制，各堵一个漏洞Four controls, each plugging one hole

    • 控制一：把初始弧长随机化。如果弧线总是从固定长度起步，那么"报告值"就和"按键次数"高度绑定。作者把初始弧长设为 0°–20° 均匀随机（Figure S5A 显示这个范围很好地覆盖了实际报告值的分布），从而削弱了报告值与运动需求之间的确定性耦合。 Control 1: randomise the starting arc. If the arc always began at a fixed length, the reported value would be tightly bound to the number of key presses. The initial length was uniformly random over 0°–20° (Figure S5A shows this range covers the observed distribution of reports well), which decouples the reported value from the motor demands.
    • 控制二：看调整时间。如果 beta 反映运动量，那它应该和"花了多久调整弧线"有关。结果没有相关：平均 r = −0.018, p = 0.63。 Control 2: check adjustment time. If beta reflected motor effort, it should relate to how long the arc took to adjust. It did not: mean r = −0.018, p = 0.63.
    • 控制三：换一种运动来报告。这是最巧妙的一项。在"位置报告期"，被试是用鼠标点击来报告位置的——一个与"按键调弧长"完全不同的动作。如果 beta 只是按键相关，它在这个阶段就不该有预测力。结果 beta 在这个阶段依然能预测不确定性：t(13) = 3.75, p = 0.002（Figure S5B）。实际上这是全文第二强的效应。 Control 3: use a different movement. The cleverest one. During the location-report period participants reported with a mouse click — a movement entirely different from the key presses used to adjust the arc. If beta were merely key-press-related it should have no predictive power here. It did: t(13) = 3.75, p = 0.002 (Figure S5B) — in fact the second strongest effect in the paper.
    • 控制四：眼动。用注视位置、注视速度、瞳孔大小这五个眼动特征去预测报告的不确定性。结果预测力很弱且在所有检验的时间点都不显著，远不如 beta 频段（Figure S5C）。 Control 4: eye movements. Five eye features — gaze position, gaze velocity, pupil size — were used to predict reported uncertainty. Predictive power was weak and non-significant at every time point tested, far below the beta band (Figure S5C).

    作者的结论措辞很克制：这些结果"反对 beta 活动仅仅由不确定性报告相关的运动反应驱动的解释"，转而支持它反映"一个与主观不确定性或信心耦合的决策相关内部状态"。注意 "solely"（仅仅）这个词——他们承认运动成分可能存在，只是不是全部。 The authors' conclusion is carefully worded: these results "argue against the interpretation that the beta-band activity is solely driven by the motor responses related to uncertainty reports", and instead support "a decision-related internal state coupled with subjective uncertainty or confidence". Note the word "solely" — they concede a motor component may exist, just not the whole story.

    这一章教你的通用技能The transferable skill here

    任何"脑信号 ↔ 行为报告"的研究都要面对这个问题：报告本身是一个动作，动作会在脑电里留下痕迹。作者的应对方式是换一种动作去做同一件事（控制三）——这是排除运动混淆最有力的设计，比单纯统计控制有效得多。你在读其他 EEG/fMRI 论文、或者设计自己的实验时，可以直接借用这个思路。Any study linking a brain signal to a behavioural report faces this: the report is an action, and actions leave traces in EEG. The authors' answer is to do the same job with a different movement (control 3) — the strongest available design against a motor confound, far better than statistical control alone. Borrow this when reading other EEG/fMRI work, or when designing your own.

    讨论：这两个信号意味着什么Discussion: what the two signals mean

    alpha：抑制性门控，与 fMRI 互补Alpha: gating by inhibition, complementary to fMRI

    alpha 功率被广泛解释为一种抑制性调节，通过改变皮层兴奋性来门控信息流（Jensen & Mazaheri 2010 等）。有一条经典的证据支持这个看法：alpha 功率与 BOLD 信号在刺激编码期呈负相关（Hermes 等 2017）。 Alpha power is widely read as an inhibitory modulation that gates information flow by shifting cortical excitability (Jensen & Mazaheri 2010 and others). A classic piece of supporting evidence: alpha power is negatively related to the BOLD response during stimulus encoding (Hermes et al. 2017).

    作者由此把本文和此前的 fMRI 工作接成一条链：他们在 Neuron (2021)、Science Advances (2025) 和 PNAS (2026) 的工作显示，视觉与顶叶皮层的 BOLD 活动重建出的工作记忆表征同时预测记忆报告和不确定性。本文表明同样的概率信息也表达在 alpha 频段里——这意味着这种"内容与不确定性联合编码"的群体编码方案，跨测量模态（fMRI / EEG）、跨时间尺度（BOLD 的慢变化 / alpha 的毫秒级振荡）都成立。 The authors use this to chain the present work to their earlier fMRI studies: work in Neuron (2021), Science Advances (2025) and PNAS (2026) showed that working memory representations reconstructed from BOLD activity in visual and parietal cortex predict both memory reports and uncertainty. This paper shows the same probabilistic information is also expressed in the alpha band — meaning this joint content-and-uncertainty population code holds across measurement modalities (fMRI / EEG) and across temporal scales (slow BOLD / millisecond alpha).

    beta：维持现状，还是也携带内容？Beta: maintaining the status quo, or carrying content too?

    关于 beta 有两大传统解释，本文试图调和它们： There are two traditional accounts of beta, and the paper tries to reconcile them:

    • "维持现状"说（Engel & Fries 2010）：beta 与稳定当前运动状态、抑制行动计划的改变有关；这一思路被推广到认知领域，认为 beta 支持当前认知状态的稳定或执行自上而下的控制。The "status quo" account (Engel & Fries 2010): beta stabilises the current motor state and suppresses changes in action plans; extended to cognition, beta is thought to stabilise ongoing cognitive states or serve top-down control.
    • "内容特异"说（Spitzer & Haegens 2017 等）：beta 也携带关于工作记忆项目的参数化特征信息。The content-specific account (Spitzer & Haegens 2017 and others): beta also carries parametric feature information about items held in working memory.

    作者的调和方式是重新定义"状态"："这里的'状态'概念并不是一个没有内容的独立模式。就像决策计算模型里许多潜变量一样，这个状态携带一个特定的数值，很像工作记忆中维持的内容（比如物体的颜色或朝向）。在我们这里，被维持的数值就是不确定性的大小本身。" 换句话说，"状态"和"内容"不是对立的——一个持续的状态照样可以承载一个具体的量。 The reconciliation redefines "state": "the notion of 'state' here is not a distinct mode devoid of content. Rather, like many latent variables in computational models of decision-making, the state carries a specific numerical value, much like the content maintained in working memory (e.g., the color or orientation of an object). In the present case, the maintained value is the magnitude of uncertainty itself." In other words, "state" and "content" are not opposites — a persistent state can still carry a specific quantity.

    作者自己提出的替代解释The alternative explanation the authors raise themselves

    一篇好论文的局限往往不在它承认了什么，而在它没说什么。下面分成两部分：作者自己承认的，和你要自己想到的。 A good paper's limitations often lie less in what it admits than in what it leaves unsaid. Split into two: what the authors concede, and what you should notice yourself.

    作者承认的What the authors concede

    • beta 可能反映映射函数而非不确定性本身——见上一章，且作者明说难以实证区分。Beta may reflect the mapping function rather than uncertainty itself — see the previous chapter; the authors say outright that the two are hard to distinguish empirically.

    你需要自己注意到的What you should notice yourself

    • 样本量小且是单中心。14 名被试，全部来自一个实验室的招募。所有推断统计都是组水平的 t(13)。这个样本量在 EEG 研究里不算异常，但意味着个体差异基本没有被考察——比如是否存在"alpha 派"和"beta 派"的被试。原文只在 Figure S1 给了个体解码结果。Small, single-site sample.     为什么记忆的"准确度"和"精度"不是一回事？本文关心的是哪一个？Why are accuracy and precision different, and which does the paper care about?
    要点Key points

    准确度是记忆分布的中心离真实值有多远，可以有方向性偏差；精度是分布本身有多集中。可以又准又不精（中心对，但很散），也可以又偏又精（很集中，但系统性偏离）。本文关心精度，因为"散得开"正是不确定性的数学含义，而后验分布的宽度就是精度的直接度量。Accuracy is how far the centre of the memory distribution is from the truth, and can be directional; precision is how concentrated the distribution is. You can be accurate but imprecise (centred right, very spread) or biased but precise (tight but systematically off). The paper cares about precision, because "spread out" is what uncertainty means mathematically, and posterior width measures precision directly.

    为什么被试会"诚实地"报告自己的不确定性？这个设计为什么重要？Why would participants honestly report their uncertainty, and why does that design matter?
    要点Key points

    因为奖励规则让诚实成为最优解：得分是 100e−0.08d，弧越长得分越低，但只有真值落在弧内才得分。画太短会经常得零分，画太长则每题只得很少。期望收益最大化的策略恰好是按真实不确定性来定弧长——这叫激励相容设计。没有这一层，弧长就只是一个任意的数字，无法当作不确定性的有效指标。Because the reward rule makes honesty optimal: points are 100e−0.08d, decreasing in arc length, but nothing is awarded unless the true location falls inside. Too short and you often score zero; too long and you score very little each time. Expected payoff is maximised exactly by setting the arc to your true uncertainty — an incentive-compatible design. Without it, arc length would be an arbitrary number, not a valid index of uncertainty.

    贝叶斯解码器给出两个输出，分别是什么？本文怎么用它们？The Bayesian decoder gives two outputs. What are they and how are they used?
    要点Key points

    后验分布的均值作为"解码出的记忆位置"，标准差作为"解码出的不确定性"（神经不确定性指标）。均值用来和行为记忆误差比较（验证解码器确实还原了记忆内容），标准差用来和被试报告的不确定性做相关（验证神经不确定性预测主观不确定性）。The posterior mean is the decoded memory location; the posterior standard deviation is the decoded uncertainty (the neural uncertainty index). The mean is compared with behavioural memory error (verifying the decoder really recovered content); the SD is correlated with reported uncertainty (testing whether neural uncertainty predicts subjective uncertainty).

    "解码不确定性越大、解码误差也越大"这个结果为什么是好事？Why is it good news that larger decoded uncertainty goes with larger decoding error?
    要点Key points

    因为一个真正在做概率推断的解码器应该"知道自己什么时候不可靠"。如果它输出的不确定性完全是噪声，就会和它自己的实际误差无关。观察到二者相关（t(13) = 3.16, p = 0.008），说明这个后验宽度是有意义的概率量，而不只是一个数字。原文称之为"行为良好的概率解码器的标志"。Because a decoder genuinely doing probabilistic inference should "know when it is unreliable". If its stated uncertainty were noise, it would bear no relation to its own error. Observing the correlation (t(13) = 3.16, p = 0.008) shows the posterior width is a meaningful probabilistic quantity, not just a number. The paper calls it "a signature for a well-behaved probabilistic decoder".

    作者用什么证据说明 beta 不是"对刺激的反应"？What evidence shows beta is not a response to the stimulus?
    要点Key points

    两条。第一，逐时间点分析显示 beta 的预测力在刺激出现之前就已经高于机会水平，并持续整个试次（Figure 4D）——反应不可能早于刺激。第二，用当前试次的 beta 去预测上一试次的不确定性报告也成立（Figure 3D），这在因果方向上只能是"持续状态"而非"即时反应"。再加上行为上存在序列依赖（Figure 3C），三者互相印证。Two. First, the time-resolved analysis shows beta's predictive power is already above chance before stimulus onset and persists across the whole trial (Figure 4D) — a response cannot precede its stimulus. Second, current-trial beta predicts the previous trial's uncertainty report (Figure 3D), which causally can only mean a persistent state rather than an online response. Together with the behavioural serial dependence (Figure 3C), the three converge.

    "beta 只是按键运动"这个质疑为什么危险？作者怎么排除它？Why is the "beta is just key presses" objection dangerous, and how is it addressed?
    要点Key points

    危险在于 beta 的皮层来源包含中央前/后沟（感觉运动皮层），而报告弧长确实需要按键——如果 beta 只反映运动准备，第二条主线就垮了。四项控制：(1) 初始弧长 0–20° 均匀随机，切断报告值与运动量的确定性耦合；(2) 弧长与调整时间无相关（r = −0.018, p = 0.63）；(3) 换用鼠标点击的位置报告期，beta 依然能预测不确定性（t(13) = 3.75, p = 0.002）——这是最有力的一项，因为动作类型完全不同；(4) 眼动指标预测力弱且不显著。作者措辞谨慎，只说反对"仅仅"由运动驱动。It is dangerous because beta's cortical sources include the pre/postcentral sulcus (sensorimotor cortex) and reporting arc length does require key presses — if beta only reflects motor preparation, the second main claim collapses. Four controls: (1) initial arc uniformly random over 0–20°, breaking the deterministic coupling between reported value and motor demand; (2) no correlation between arc length and adjustment time (r = −0.018, p = 0.63); (3) during the mouse-click location-report period, beta still predicts uncertainty (t(13) = 3.75, p = 0.002) — the strongest check, since the movement is entirely different; (4) eye features weak and non-significant. The authors word this carefully, arguing only against solely motor-driven accounts.

    为什么作者说 alpha 和 beta 构成"双重分离"？说出至少三个维度上的分离。Why a double dissociation? Name at least three dimensions of separation.
    要点Key points

    分离体现在：(1) 功能——alpha 解码内容且其精度预测不确定性，beta 预测不确定性的数值但不与内容相关；(2) 时间——alpha 的相关只在延迟期中后段一个短窗口显著，beta 全程高于机会水平、刺激前就有；(3) 空间——alpha 与目标位置相关的源在左侧顶枕沟（V3AB/IPS0），beta 与报告弧长相关的源在中央前/后沟及若干内侧区；而且 alpha 没有任何源与报告弧长相关。三个维度上互不重叠，才叫双重分离。Separation appears in: (1) function — alpha decodes content and its precision predicts uncertainty; beta predicts the uncertainty value but is unrelated to content; (2) time — alpha's correlation is significant only in a short mid-to-late delay window, while beta is above chance throughout and before stimulus onset; (3) space — alpha's location-related source sits on the left parieto-occipital sulcus (V3AB/IPS0), beta's uncertainty-related sources at the pre/postcentral sulci and medial regions, with no alpha source correlating with reported arc length at all. Non-overlap on all three is what makes it a double dissociation.

    作者提出的"替代解释"是什么？他们为什么说它难以排除？What alternative do the authors raise, and why is it hard to rule out?
    要点Key points

    替代解释：beta 反映的可能是"信心标准 / 映射函数"的波动，而不是记忆不确定性本身。因为被试必须把内部证据通过一套标准转换成外显的弧长，报告的数值同时包含了不确定性估计和映射过程。作者说这两种解释"会预期产生高度相似的行为和神经特征，因此难以实证区分"——也就是说，即使再补数据，纯相关的方法也分不开。The alternative: beta may reflect fluctuations in the confidence criterion / mapping function rather than mnemonic uncertainty itself. Participants must convert internal evidence into an explicit arc length through a criterion, so the reported value contains both the uncertainty estimate and the mapping. The authors say the two accounts "would be expected to produce highly similar behavioral and neural signatures, making them difficult to distinguish empirically" — meaning more correlational data would not separate them.

    本文最根本的方法论局限是什么？如果要你做一个后续实验，你会怎么做？What is the deepest methodological limitation, and what follow-up would you run?
    要点Key points

    最根本的是全部为相关证据，没有因果操纵。所有结论都建立在"神经活动 X 与行为 Y 相关"上，而 X 与 Y 可能同时受第三个变量影响（例如注意波动、唤醒水平）。可行的后续：用节律性 TMS 或经颅交流电刺激（tACS）分别扰动 alpha 与 beta 频段，看不确定性报告是否选择性地改变——如果干扰 beta 只影响报告的"整体水平"而不影响逐试次的记忆精度，就能把标量信心信号和表征精度真正分开。另一个方向是用公开数据（osf.io/t96rn）检验 alpha 与 beta 冲突时的报告行为。The deepest one is that all evidence is correlational, with no causal manipulation. Every conclusion rests on "neural activity X correlates with behaviour Y", and X and Y may both be driven by a third variable such as attentional or arousal fluctuations. A feasible follow-up: use rhythmic TMS or tACS to perturb alpha and beta separately and see whether uncertainty reports change selectively — if perturbing beta shifts only the overall level of reports without affecting trial-wise memory precision, the scalar confidence signal and representational precision would be genuinely separated. Another route: use the public data (osf.io/t96rn) to test reporting behaviour when alpha and beta conflict.

    为什么作者把 beta 称为"状态"却又说它"不是没有内容的"？Why call beta a "state" yet insist it is "not devoid of content"?
    要点Key points

    为了调和 beta 研究里的两大传统："维持现状"说（beta 稳定当前状态、抑制改变）和"内容特异"说（beta 也携带工作记忆项目的特征信息）。作者的论点是这两个说法并不冲突：一个持续的状态照样可以承载一个具体的数值。就像决策计算模型里的潜变量，"状态"本身就有取值——只不过本文中这个取值是"不确定性的大小"，而不是物体的颜色或朝向。所以 beta 既是一个跨试次持续的"状态"，又携带了一个有具体数值的"内容"。To reconcile beta's two traditional accounts: the "status quo" view (beta stabilises the current state and suppresses change) and the content-specific view (beta also carries feature information about remembered items). The authors argue these do not conflict: a persistent state can still carry a specific value. Like a latent variable in a computational decision model, a "state" has a value — here that value is the magnitude of uncertainty rather than an object's colour or orientation. Beta is thus both a trial-spanning state and the carrier of a content with a definite value.
